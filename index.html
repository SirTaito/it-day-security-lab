Level 1: Social Media Leak Attempts: 0

Mission: Look at Tom's public Instagram profile shown on the projector. Combine his personal details to guess his personal email password.

Hint 1: Check his Instagram bio for his dog's name and his birth year.
Hint 2: Format is [DogName][BirthYear]! (Capital first letter + exclamation mark).
Level 2: Corporate Admin Attempts: 0

Mission: Tom uses a stricter password policy for his work account at NordicTech. Check his LinkedIn & company profile on the screen to crack his admin login.

Hint 1 (OSINT): Look at his LinkedIn location (city) and the year he joined NordicTech.
Hint 2 (Leetspeak): Tom replaces the first lowercase vowel in the city name with @.
Hint 3 (Policy Rule): Structure is [City with @][JoinYear]# (12 characters total).
Level 3: Passkey Protection FIDO2 / WebAuthn

Account Upgraded: Tom removed his password completely and enabled a Passkey. Even though you know his birthday, dog, city, and email, there is no password left to guess.

Your Security Takeaway Checklist

  • 1. Ditch personal clues: Never use pets, birthdays, cities, or years (2026!) in passwords.
  • 2. Enable Passkeys first: Activate Passkeys on Google, Apple, Microsoft & Amazon—only your physical device can unlock your account.
  • 3. Use a Password Manager: Where Passkeys aren't supported yet, generate 16+ random characters (1 account = 1 unique password).
  • 4. Use a 4-Word Passphrase: For your Master Password, combine 4 random words (e.g., Coffee-Beaver-Table-Cloud!).
